1. Developer and contact

Cozy Zombie Odyssey is an Android game developed by Lorenzo Gaetano Lollai, the controller responsible for the processing described in this policy. For support, privacy questions or data requests, email lollaideveloper@gmail.com.

2. Game data stored on your device

The game stores progress and settings on your device: currencies, upgrades, weapons, completed runs and eras, unlocked items, rewards, reward cooldowns, and music and sound preferences. These records restore progress and apply rewards consistently.

The game has no player account, sign-in or developer-operated cloud save. Saved progress is not uploaded to a developer server. The game does not request your name or email to play, or access GPS, contacts, camera or microphone. Device and operating-system backups depend on your own device settings.

3. Support correspondence

If you contact the developer, your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide are used to answer and resolve your request. Please send only information relevant to the problem. The support mailbox uses Gmail; Google processes delivery and storage under its Privacy Policy. Contacting support does not subscribe you to marketing.

4. Google AdMob advertising — planned

Future advertising-enabled versions will offer optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob and its Google Play services components. A rewarded video starts after you choose the offer, and its game reward is described before that choice.

When the advertising SDK requests or displays an ad, Google may process the following:

  • An IP address, which may be used to estimate an approximate location.
  • App and advertisement interactions, such as launches, taps and video views.
  • Technical information about device and app performance.
  • Identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, app set ID and other identifiers applicable to the SDK and your settings.

This processing supports advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. It can occur when an ad is requested or loaded, even if you do not finish watching a video. These data can identify a device or relate to an individual; they are not necessarily anonymous. Google's SDK transmits user data using TLS. See Google's advertising SDK data disclosure and how Google uses information from partner apps.

5. Advertising choices

The planned advertising release will present required consent choices before requesting ads and offer a way to reopen privacy choices where required. Those controls are not present in the current preview. Consent to watch a rewarded video is separate from consent to personalised advertising or access to device identifiers.

You can decline or withdraw consent where consent is requested. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully carried out beforehand. Playing the game does not require agreeing to personalised advertising. The availability and type of rewarded advertisements may depend on your choices, age-related restrictions and applicable requirements. Non-personalised ads may still process data and require consent for certain purposes.

Android's privacy settings also provide controls for the advertising ID. Changing those settings does not automatically delete data already held by providers. See Google's advertising and device controls.

6. Unity and earlier previews

The game is built with Unity. Unity Engine Diagnostics has been disabled in the project for subsequent builds. Unity Analytics, Unity Ads and Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics are not used in the current game.

Earlier preview builds were made with Unity Engine Diagnostics enabled and could have sent installation identifiers and technical diagnostic records to Unity to investigate errors and stability. Disabling the setting does not modify an APK already installed or erase previously transmitted records. Actual collection from those previews has not been confirmed.

For this service, Unity acts as the developer's processor and documents a default retention of 90 days. If you have a concern about an earlier preview, email the developer with the app version and relevant details. Requests concerning provider-held records may require an installation identifier and assistance from Unity. See Unity Diagnostics privacy information and Unity's policy for game players.

7. Purposes and legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, local game records are used to provide the game functions you request (Article 6(1)(b), where personal data are involved). Support correspondence is processed for the legitimate interest in responding to requests and resolving problems (Article 6(1)(f)), or to comply with a legal obligation when handling statutory requests (Article 6(1)(c)). Earlier preview diagnostics, if collected, serve the legitimate interest in investigating game errors and stability (Article 6(1)(f)).

In advertising-enabled versions, consent is the basis for advertising processing that requires it (Article 6(1)(a)), including personalised advertising and device access where applicable. Necessary advertising security and fraud-prevention processing may rely on legitimate interests or legal obligations where permitted. Google explains its own purposes and legal bases in its privacy information. Merely continuing to play does not provide consent.

9. Retention and deletion

Game records remain on your device until removed. To reset them, open Android Settings, select Cozy Zombie Odyssey under Apps, then clear the app's storage/data. This removes local progress, preferences and the game's local backup files. Device backup copies follow your operating-system settings.

Support messages are retained only as long as needed to resolve the request, handle necessary follow-up and meet applicable legal obligations or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Uninstalling the game does not delete support messages or records previously sent to a provider.

Google's retention depends on the type of data, its purpose and applicable settings; see Google's retention information. Unity's documented diagnostic default is described above. Provider retention is not controlled by clearing the game's local storage.

10. Your rights

Depending on the applicable law and circumstances, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or withdraw consent. Email lollaideveloper@gmail.com. The developer may ask for information reasonably needed to identify the relevant records and verify the request. There is no game account to delete.

GDPR requests normally receive a response within one month; extensions for complex requests follow the applicable notice requirements. You may complain to your local supervisory authority, including Italy's Garante per la protezione dei dati personali. See the Garante's explanation of your rights.

Requests about data controlled independently by Google or GitHub can also be made through the contact and privacy controls in their linked policies.

11. Young players

Cozy Zombie Odyssey is intended for teenagers and adults aged 13 and older and is not designed for children under 13. This intended audience is separate from the content rating assigned by an app store. The current preview does not include an age check.

Being 13 or older does not always mean being old enough to consent independently to data processing. Requirements vary by country. Before advertising is activated, the release will apply the relevant protections for minors and consent requirements, including parental authorisation where required, rather than treating every player as an adult. Personalised advertising is restricted for young players under Google's applicable age treatment.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal data in connection with the game, contact lollaideveloper@gmail.com so the concern and any necessary deletion request can be addressed.

This policy will be updated when the game's data practices change, including before an advertising-enabled release. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated and new consent requested where required.
