1. Developer and contact

Cozy Zombie Odyssey is an Android game developed by Lorenzo Gaetano Lollai, the controller responsible for the processing described in this policy. For support, privacy questions or data requests, email lollaideveloper@gmail.com.

2. Game data stored on your device

The game stores progress and settings on your device: currencies, upgrades, weapons, completed runs and eras, unlocked items, rewards, reward cooldowns, and music and sound preferences. These records restore progress and apply rewards consistently.

The game has no player account, sign-in or developer-operated cloud save. Saved progress is not uploaded to a developer server. The game does not request your name or email to play, or access GPS, contacts, camera or microphone. Device and operating-system backups depend on your own device settings.

3. Support correspondence

If you contact the developer, your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide are used to answer and resolve your request. Please send only information relevant to the problem. The support mailbox uses Gmail; Google processes delivery and storage under its Privacy Policy. Contacting support does not subscribe you to marketing.

4. Optional Google AdMob advertisements

Advertising-enabled versions offer optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob and its Google Play services components. The game describes the reward before you choose to watch. Videos are shown only after you select an offer; starting or retrying a game does not require watching a video.

Version 0.50.0 is a phone-test preview using Google's sample ad units. A later release may use the game's own AdMob units. Test mode concerns ad delivery and revenue; it does not mean the advertising SDK is absent or that every privacy-related request is offline. See Google's explanation of test advertisements.

When the advertising SDK requests or displays an ad, Google may process the following:

  • An IP address, which may be used to estimate an approximate location.
  • App and advertisement interactions, such as launches, taps and video views.
  • Technical information about device and app performance.
  • Identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, app set ID and other identifiers applicable to the SDK and your settings.

This processing supports advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. It can occur when an ad is requested or loaded, even if you do not finish watching a video. These data can identify a device or relate to an individual; they are not necessarily anonymous. Google's SDK transmits user data using TLS. See Google's advertising SDK data disclosure and how Google uses information from partner apps.

5. Advertising choices

At launch, Google's UMP SDK checks whether a privacy message is required. The game presents the required Google consent form before requesting advertisements. Privacy requests themselves may communicate with Google. Where Google requires a privacy options entry point, open the game's Settings and choose PRIVACY CHOICES to review or change your choices. The game uses the SDK's current consent status to determine whether advertisements may be requested. See Google's UMP information.

Choosing to watch a rewarded video is separate from consenting to personalised advertising or access to device identifiers. Your privacy choices are stored by the Google SDK; local game records separately track rewards and cooldowns.

You can decline or withdraw consent where consent is requested. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully carried out beforehand. Playing the game does not require agreeing to personalised advertising. The availability and type of rewarded advertisements may depend on your choices, age-related restrictions and applicable requirements. Non-personalised ads may still process data and require consent for certain purposes.

Android's privacy settings also provide controls for the advertising ID. Changing those settings does not automatically delete data already held by providers. See Google's advertising and device controls.

6. Unity and earlier previews

The game is built with Unity. Unity Engine Diagnostics is disabled in the advertising-enabled preview starting with version 0.50.0. Unity Analytics, Unity Ads and Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics are not used in the current game. This is separate from the diagnostic information processed by Google's advertising SDK described above.

Earlier preview builds were made with Unity Engine Diagnostics enabled and could have sent installation identifiers and technical diagnostic records to Unity to investigate errors and stability. Disabling the setting does not modify an APK already installed or erase previously transmitted records. Actual collection from those previews has not been confirmed.

For this service, Unity acts as the developer's processor and documents a default retention of 90 days. If you have a concern about an earlier preview, email the developer with the app version and relevant details. Requests concerning provider-held records may require an installation identifier and assistance from Unity. See Unity Diagnostics privacy information and Unity's policy for game players.

9. Retention and deletion

Game records remain on your device until removed. To reset them, open Android Settings, select Cozy Zombie Odyssey under Apps, then clear the app's storage/data. This removes local progress, preferences and the game's local backup files. Device backup copies follow your operating-system settings.

The next build will also provide Settings → DELETE DATA → DELETE LOCAL DATA, with a confirmation and a cancel option. This control is not included in preview version 0.50.0. It resets progress, coins, gems, upgrades and sound settings on the current device and removes the game's local save backups. Locally stored purchased currency is also lost. It does not erase advertising records held by Google, change Google privacy choices, or remove operating-system backup copies.

Support messages are retained only as long as needed to resolve the request, handle necessary follow-up and meet applicable legal obligations or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Uninstalling the game does not delete support messages or records previously sent to a provider.

Google's retention depends on the type of data, its purpose and applicable settings; see Google's retention information. Unity's documented diagnostic default is described above. Provider retention is not controlled by clearing the game's local storage.

10. Your rights

For personal data held by the developer, such as support emails and attachments, contact lollaideveloper@gmail.com. Requests about data controlled independently by Google or GitHub can also be made through the contact and privacy controls in their linked policies.

11. Young players

The planned public release of Cozy Zombie Odyssey is intended for teenagers and adults aged 13 and over, corresponding to Google Play's 13–15, 16–17 and 18-and-over target groups. The game is not directed to children under 13. Target-audience age groups are separate from the content ratings assigned in individual countries. Availability for a particular player remains subject to those ratings, applicable local requirements and parental or store controls.

Current test previews. The advertising-enabled previews described above do not ask for or store a player's age or date of birth. They currently use the standard UMP consent flow and a maximum ad content rating of T. They do not apply age-specific child or teen advertising treatment. An ad content rating is not proof of a player's age or ability to consent, and Google's advertising consent form is not an age verification system. Existing advertising data processing is described in sections 4 and 5; changing the target audience in Play Console does not change an installed preview's behaviour.

Public-release checks. Age-related advertising and data protections, including requirements affecting teenagers, must be verified and applied before the monetised public release. The planned audience does not mean that all players are adults or can independently consent in every country. These checks are not a statement that all age-related release requirements have been met by the current previews. This policy will be updated before changes to the game's age-related controls or data processing take effect. See Google Play's target-audience guidance and Google's age-related advertising settings.

Requirements for independent consent and parental authorisation vary by country. Where applicable law requires parental authorisation for personal-data processing, that authorisation must be obtained before the processing. The current previews do not implement a parental-consent mechanism. Choosing to watch a rewarded video or selecting a target-audience age group does not provide parental authorisation.

If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about your child's data, or want to request access, correction or deletion of personal data held by the developer, contact lollaideveloper@gmail.com. The developer may request information reasonably necessary to identify the relevant records and verify your authority to make the request. Local game records and independently controlled provider records can also be managed as described in sections 9 and 10.
