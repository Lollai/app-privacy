1. Developer and contact

Cozy Zombie Odyssey is an Android game developed by Lorenzo Gaetano Lollai, the controller responsible for the processing described in this policy. For support, privacy questions or data requests, email lollaideveloper@gmail.com.

2. Game data stored on your device

The game stores progress and settings on your device: currencies, upgrades, weapons, completed runs and eras, unlocked items, rewards, reward cooldowns, and music and sound preferences. It also stores checksums of delivered purchases to avoid crediting the same purchase twice. These records restore progress and apply purchases and rewards consistently.

The update uses Google Play Games authentication and Saved Games to back up progress with Google. It associates a Play Games player ID with saved progress, currencies, upgrades, reward records and purchase checksums. It retrieves these records on another device using the same Play Games account. No developer-operated cloud-save server is used, and no Google account password or OAuth client secret is stored in the game. Sound and vibration preferences stay on the device. The game does not request your name or email to play, or access GPS, contacts, camera or microphone. Device and operating-system backups depend on your own device settings.

The Play Games SDK also processes service analytics and diagnostic information for stability and product improvements, as explained in Google's Play Games data disclosure. The game does not request your friends list or upload gameplay screenshots. Google handles Play Games authentication and storage under its Privacy Policy. The game reads an existing save before uploading local progress, and does not automatically combine saves from different Play Games accounts. Offline progress stays on the device until a later successful sync.

4. Optional Google AdMob advertisements

Advertising-enabled versions offer optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob and its Google Play services components. The game describes the reward before you choose to watch. Videos are shown only after you select an offer; starting or retrying a game does not require watching a video.

Version 0.58.0 is configured to request advertisements through the game's own AdMob units. Ad availability depends on Google, your privacy choices and applicable restrictions. Earlier test previews, including version 0.50.0, use Google's sample advertisements. Test advertisements do not generate advertising revenue, but privacy checks, SDK initialisation and ad requests can still involve data processing. See Google's explanation of test advertisements.

When the advertising SDK requests or displays an ad, Google may process the following:

  • An IP address, which may be used to estimate an approximate location.
  • App and advertisement interactions, such as launches, taps and video views.
  • Technical information about device and app performance.
  • Identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers associated with signed-in accounts on the device.

This processing supports advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. It can occur when an ad is requested or loaded, even if you do not finish watching a video. These data can identify a device or relate to an individual; they are not necessarily anonymous. Google's SDK transmits user data using TLS. See Google's advertising SDK data disclosure and how Google uses information from partner apps.

5. Advertising choices

At launch, Google's UMP SDK checks whether a privacy message is required. The game presents the required Google consent form before requesting advertisements. Privacy requests themselves may communicate with Google. Where Google requires a privacy options entry point, open the game's Settings and choose PRIVACY CHOICES to review or change your choices. The game uses the SDK's current consent status to determine whether advertisements may be requested. See Google's UMP information.

Choosing to watch a rewarded video is separate from consenting to personalised advertising or access to device identifiers. Your privacy choices are stored by the Google SDK; local game records separately track rewards and cooldowns.

You can decline or withdraw consent where consent is requested. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully carried out beforehand. Playing the game does not require agreeing to personalised advertising. The availability and type of rewarded advertisements may depend on your choices, age-related restrictions and applicable requirements. Non-personalised ads may still process data and require consent for certain purposes.

Android's privacy settings also provide controls for the advertising ID. Changing those settings does not automatically delete data already held by providers. See Google's advertising and device controls.

6. Google Play purchases

The game offers optional one-time purchases of coin and gem packs through Google Play Billing. Google Play presents the price and payment confirmation. Google processes the payment using your Google account and payment method. The game does not receive your full card number, bank account details or Google account password through the billing integration. Google's processing is described in its Privacy Policy and Google Payments Privacy Notice.

The game receives purchase information from Google Play, including the product, purchase status, quantity, signed purchase data and a purchase token. It uses this information to verify a purchase, credit the purchased currency, recover purchases whose delivery has not finished and confirm delivery to Google Play. Purchase tokens and signed receipt data are processed in memory; the game saves currency and purchase checksums on the device to prevent duplicate delivery. The game does not send these records to a developer-operated server.

Google may also make order and sales information available to the developer through Play Console or payment reports. Depending on the record, this can include an order identifier, product, amount, purchase or refund status, and regional or tax information. The developer uses available transaction records for purchase support, refunds, accounting, fraud prevention and applicable legal obligations. If you contact support about a purchase, you may provide an order identifier to help locate it. Please do not send card details or your account password.

Purchased coins and gems are consumable items recorded in the game save. The latest save successfully synchronised with Google Play Games can restore the remaining balance and purchase checksums on another device. Google Play order history alone cannot recreate balances already delivered and spent. Unsynchronised changes and older purchases without a surviving save cannot be recovered automatically. If two devices have conflicting progress, the player chooses one complete save; balances and separate purchases are not added together. Local recovery copies retain the alternatives on the device where the conflict was resolved. Deleting local data does not delete Google's order history, cancel an order or request a refund. For a purchase problem, contact the developer or use Google Play's refund support.

7. Unity and earlier previews

The game is built with Unity. Unity Engine Diagnostics is disabled in version 0.58.0 and in advertising-enabled previews starting with version 0.50.0. Unity Analytics, Unity Ads and Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics are not used in the current game. This is separate from the diagnostic information processed by Google's advertising SDK described above.

Earlier preview builds were made with Unity Engine Diagnostics enabled and could have sent installation identifiers and technical diagnostic records to Unity to investigate errors and stability. Disabling the setting does not modify an APK already installed or erase previously transmitted records. Actual collection from those previews has not been confirmed.

For this service, Unity acts as the developer's processor and documents a default retention of 90 days. If you have a concern about an earlier preview, email the developer with the app version and relevant details. Requests concerning provider-held records may require an installation identifier and assistance from Unity. See Unity Diagnostics privacy information and Unity's policy for game players.

10. Retention and deletion

Game records remain on your device until removed. To reset them, open Android Settings, select Cozy Zombie Odyssey under Apps, then clear the app's storage/data. This removes local progress, preferences and the game's local backup files. Device backup copies follow your operating-system settings.

In version 0.58.0, open Settings → DELETE DATA → DELETE LOCAL DATA, then confirm to reset the game. You can cancel before deletion. This resets progress, coins, gems, upgrades, sound settings and purchase checksums on the current device and removes the game's local save backups. Changes that were not synchronised are lost. This control also deletes local conflict recovery copies and pauses automatic cloud sync. It does not delete the Google Play Games save. Reconnecting using CLOUD SAVE can restore that Google save. The control is unavailable while a game, video or unresolved purchase is in progress. It does not erase advertising or payment records held by Google, change Google privacy choices, request a refund, or remove operating-system backup copies.

Support messages are retained only as long as needed to resolve the request, handle necessary follow-up and meet applicable legal obligations or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Uninstalling the game does not delete support messages or records previously sent to a provider.

Purchase checksums remain in the local save until it is removed and, when synchronised, in the Google Play Games save until that game data is deleted through Google. Local recovery copies are removed by DELETE LOCAL DATA. The developer retains any copies of transaction records for support or accounting only as long as necessary for those purposes and applicable legal obligations or claims. Google independently retains payment and order records, including records made available through Play Console, under its own policies and legal obligations; clearing the game save does not remove them.

Google's retention depends on the type of data, its purpose and applicable settings; see Google's retention information. Unity's documented diagnostic default is described above. Provider retention is not controlled by clearing the game's local storage.

12. Young players

Cozy Zombie Odyssey is intended for teenagers and adults aged 13 and over, corresponding to Google Play's 13–15, 16–17 and 18-and-over target groups. The game is not directed to children under 13. Target-audience age groups are separate from the content ratings assigned in individual countries. Availability for a particular player remains subject to those ratings, applicable local requirements and parental or store controls.

Current controls. Version 0.58.0 does not ask for or store a player's age or date of birth. It uses the standard UMP consent flow and a maximum ad content rating of T. It does not apply age-specific child or teen advertising treatment. An ad content rating is not proof of a player's age or ability to consent, and Google's advertising consent form is not an age verification system. Advertising data processing is described in sections 4 and 5; changing the target audience in Play Console does not change an installed build's behaviour.

The intended audience does not mean that all players are adults or can independently consent in every country. The controls described here do not establish that parental authorisation has been obtained. This policy will be updated before changes to the game's age-related controls or data processing take effect. See Google Play's target-audience guidance and Google's age-related advertising settings.

Requirements for independent consent and parental authorisation vary by country. Where applicable law requires parental authorisation for personal-data processing, that authorisation must be obtained before the processing. Version 0.58.0 does not implement an in-game parental-consent mechanism. Choosing to watch a rewarded video or selecting a target-audience age group does not provide parental authorisation. Google Play purchases are also subject to Google's account, payment and parental-control requirements.

If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about your child's data, or want to request access, correction or deletion of personal data held by the developer, contact lollaideveloper@gmail.com. The developer may request information reasonably necessary to identify the relevant records and verify your authority to make the request. Local game records and independently controlled provider records can also be managed as described in sections 10 and 11.
